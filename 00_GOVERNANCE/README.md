## Operating rules
1. Preserve source documents; never silently overwrite or reconcile conflicting evidence.
2. Label claims: VERIFIED, FOUNDER-REPORTED, DOCUMENTED, PROPOSED, HYPOTHESIS, UNKNOWN.
3. Every material decision has an owner, date, rationale, evidence, risks and review trigger.
4. Every requirement links to design, implementation, tests and release evidence before being called complete.
5. No AI output becomes a diagnosis, prescription or clinical decision without an explicitly approved and validated clinical workflow and accountable licensed professional.
6. No real patient data in prototypes or test fixtures unless the privacy, security, consent and governance controls are approved.
7. No production deployment, external integration, spending commitment or repository merge is implied by this document.
8. Changes are reviewed through a pull request and impact assessment.

## Evidence boundary
