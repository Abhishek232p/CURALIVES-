# Curalives Operating Cadence

## Weekly
- Product: customer evidence, top workflow friction, activation and task completion.
- Engineering: delivery, defects, test health, reliability, security changes.
- Clinical safety/privacy: unresolved hazards, data flows, consent and access exceptions.
- Commercial: discovery, pilot funnel, willingness to pay, partner dependencies.
- Founder: decisions required, capital allocation, scope changes and blockers.

## Monthly
- Reassess product-market assumptions and segment focus.
- Review architecture and technical debt.
- Review risk register and regulatory changes with qualified counsel.
- Review cash runway, gross margin assumptions and hiring/vendor commitments.
- Publish a dated operating review with evidence links.

## Stage gates
1. Problem evidence: repeated high-cost workflow pain in a defined segment.
2. Solution evidence: prototype usability and task completion with representative users.
3. Safety and privacy: hazard analysis, threat model, data map, consent and access review.
4. Pilot readiness: signed scope, training, support, incident process, metrics and rollback.
5. Commercial proof: repeat usage, retention, willingness to pay and sustainable unit economics.
6. Scale readiness: operational reliability, support capacity, compliance and governance.

## Metric discipline
Prefer outcomes over vanity metrics. Candidate measures include time to complete a clinic workflow, documentation correction rate, clinician approval rate, appointment completion, follow-up completion, weekly active clinics, retention, support burden and gross margin. Define denominators and data sources before reporting.
